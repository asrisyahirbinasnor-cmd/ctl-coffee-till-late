About Us

CTL CAFE (Coffee Till Late) is a small coffee stall. We serve fresh coffee and chocolate drinks for students and workers who need a good drink to start the day.

Why choose us?

  • Fresh drinks made to order
  • Simple and fair prices
  • Friendly service
  • Special signature drink: Butter Late

How to order

  1. Look at our menu.
  2. Go to the order page.
  3. Choose your drinks and quantity.
  4. Press the Place Order button.
